Site Name

The name of my website is Explore Jujuy. I chose this name because the website will help people discover places in Jujuy, Argentina. The name is short and tells visitors what the site is about.

The site will use a simple text logo with the name Explore Jujuy, as shown at the top of this document.

Site Purpose

The website will be a small travel guide for people visiting Jujuy for the first time. It will introduce local places, show photos, and share basic tips to help visitors plan a trip.

Planned Pages

  • Home: A short introduction to Jujuy and three featured places.
  • Places: Information about places such as Purmamarca, Tilcara, and Salinas Grandes. Visitors will be able to filter places by type and save favorites.
  • Plan Your Visit: Travel tips and a form where visitors can choose their interests and create a simple trip summary.

I will use an array of place objects to create the place cards. JavaScript functions will display the cards, filter them, and respond to the form. I will use localStorage to remember favorite places. Photos will be optimized and use lazy loading where appropriate. A references page linked in the footer will list the sources for photos and information.

Scenarios

  • Which places should I visit on my first trip to Jujuy?
  • Where can I find information about towns and natural attractions?
  • Can I save the places I want to visit and make a simple trip plan?

Color Schema

I chose colors that remind me of mountains and earth. These colors are also used in this plan.

  • Dark green (#24483E): Header, footer, headings, and buttons. Text on green will be white.
  • Clay (#98452D): Links and small accents.
  • Cream (#FAF5EB): Page background.
  • Dark gray (#292929): Paragraphs and lists.

Typography

Georgia: Headings and site name

Arial: Paragraphs, navigation, lists, and form labels. This sentence is an example of the body font.

These fonts are easy to read and do not require a separate font download.
