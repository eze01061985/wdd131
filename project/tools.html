BUILD YOUR TOOLBOX

A few tools are enough.

Start with one game engine. Add an art or audio tool when your project needs it. The descriptions below introduce what each tool does; follow the official links for tutorials and current requirements.

Explore the tools

Favorites stay in this browser. Use each card's button to save or remove a tool.

How to choose your first engine

  1. Choose the kind of game.

    A small 2D idea gives you a clear first task. Check that the engine supports the type of project you want to make.

  2. Try a short tutorial.

    Make something move and respond to input. Notice whether you prefer writing scripts or working with visual events.

  3. Stay with one tool for a small project.

    Switching engines every day makes it hard to finish. Build a tiny prototype before deciding whether a tool fits your way of learning.

Remember the assets

Images, music, and sound effects have their own licenses. Before using downloaded assets, check the permission and credit requirements. Keep a list of the creator, source link, and license with your project.
